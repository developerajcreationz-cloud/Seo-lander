FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is GoHighLevel?

GoHighLevel (HighLevel) is an all-in-one platform that combines a CRM, sales pipelines, funnels and websites, SMS and email marketing, booking calendars, reputation tools and AI assistants — so you can replace several separate tools with one system.

Do I need my own GoHighLevel account?

Yes. The platform subscription is billed by HighLevel directly. We build and manage everything inside your account, so you own your data and keep the system whether or not you keep working with us.

Can you migrate us from another CRM or email tool?

Yes. We import contacts, map fields and tags, rebuild your key automations and pipelines, and run both systems in parallel until the switch is safe.

Do you build AI chatbots and voice AI?

Yes. We set up conversation AI for chat, SMS and social messages, and voice AI for answering and qualifying calls, with clear handoff rules to your team.

How long does setup take?

A focused starter system (CRM, pipeline, lead capture and core follow-up automations) is typically live within one to two weeks. Larger builds with funnels, AI and migrations take longer and are scoped after a free audit.

Is SMS and email sending compliant?

We configure consent capture, opt-out handling, A2P 10DLC registration where required, and email authentication so your messages are delivered and compliant with the rules in your market.
