FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?

SEO earns rankings and clicks in traditional search results. AEO structures content so featured snippets, voice assistants and search features can lift a direct answer from it. GEO builds the entity authority and citations that make AI systems such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews mention and recommend your brand.

How long do results take?

Most projects see measurable movement in impressions and rankings within 60–90 days, with compounding click and lead growth across months 4–6. Timelines depend on competition, site health and content velocity.

Can you prove results?

Yes. Every engagement is reported from your own Google Search Console and analytics. Our case studies compare the last six months with the previous six, including impressions from Google’s generative AI features.

Do I need AEO and GEO if my SEO is already good?

Strong SEO is the foundation, but AI answers choose sources differently from blue-link rankings. AEO and GEO add structured answers, schema, entity consistency and third-party citations so you are visible where buyers now ask questions.

Which industries do you work with?

B2B SaaS, ecommerce, local and service businesses, healthcare and professional services. Every strategy is built around your market and buyer questions.

What does it cost?

Engagements are scoped to your goals and competition. Book a free audit and we’ll recommend a plan with a fixed monthly investment.
